## Key Takeaways

### When Signals Align (Low StdDev):
- **All Bullish (>7.0):** High conviction BUY ✅
- **All Bearish (<4.0):** High conviction SELL/AVOID ❌
- **All Neutral (4.5-6.5):** HOLD or wait for catalyst ⏸️

### When Signals Diverge (High StdDev):
- **Bullish news + Bearish smart money:** 🚨 DANGER - Avoid or exit
- **Bearish news + Bullish smart money:** 💎 OPPORTUNITY - Contrarian buy
- **Mixed with no pattern:** 🤔 RESEARCH - More analysis needed

### Signal Hierarchy (Trust Order):
1. **Institutional Activity** - Largest, most informed investors
2. **Analyst Ratings** - Professional coverage with models
3. **Earnings Revisions** - Direct fundamental changes
4. **Insider Activity** - Management knows their business
5. **News Sentiment** - Can be noisy/reactive
